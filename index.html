What the sketchbook paper takes

160–180gsm sits between a drawing pad and a watercolor block. It handles most media well; for heavy, soaking watercolor, a dedicated 300gsm paper will buckle less.

About MOFUNY

MOFUNY makes paper and pens for people who switch between media in the same session: a pencil sketch, then marker, then a wash of color on top.

We keep the range small and the colors soft, and we design each piece to be picked up every day.

Shipping, returns and privacy

Shipping

We ship to addresses in the United States. Orders leave our US warehouse within 1–2 business days and usually arrive in 3–5 business days.

Shipping is free on every order.

Returns

If something isn't right, contact us within 30 days of delivery. Unused items can be returned for a full refund; damaged or defective items are replaced or refunded at no cost to you.

Privacy and contact

Payments are processed securely by PayPal. You can pay with a PayPal account or any major card; we never see or store your card details, and we use your address only to deliver your order.
